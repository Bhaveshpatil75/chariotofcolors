**Welcome to Chariot Of Colors.**

We sell art. But not just any art—we create personalized masterpieces that tell your story. Whether you need a sketch, a canvas painting, or a unique gift for someone special, we’ve got you covered.

This platform connects you directly with passionate artists who turn blank canvases into your favorite memories.

## 🎨 What We Offer

We don't just sell "art"; we craft memories. Here’s what you can find in our studio:

- **Sketches**: Detailed pencil and charcoal work that captures the raw essence of a subject.
- **Canvas Paintings**: Stunning Oil, Acrylic, and Mixed Media pieces on high-quality canvas.
- **Birthday Gifts**: Fun, personalized pop-art and caricatures that make for unforgettable presents.
- **Wedding Gifts**: Romantic couple portraits and sceneries to celebrate love.
- **Home Decorations**: Large-scale abstract and modern art to transform your living space.

## ✨ Experience the Magic

### 🎬 Poetry in Motion
Forget static images. Our **Cinematic Gallery** lets you experience the collection in motion. It’s a film-roll of art that flows across your screen, giving every masterpiece the spotlight it deserves.

### 🤖 Violet is Here to Help
Not sure what you're looking for? Just ask **Violet**. She’s our AI art consultant (powered by the smart folks at Google Gemini) who lives right in the chat window. Whether you need gift ideas or just want to chat about art styles, she’s always around.

### 🖌️ Your Vision, Our Brush
Got a specific idea? A dream you want painted? You can **Book an Order** directly with us. Tell us what you need, and we’ll handle the rest.

### 🤝 We Keep It Real
We value authenticity. Our **Reviews** are from real people, and we have a dedicated **Complaint Portal** because we actually care if something goes wrong. We want you to be happy with your art.
